❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my rank?

Ranks are usually delivered within a few minutes after a successful payment. If you don't receive your rank within 10 minutes, please open a support ticket in our Discord.

Can I get a refund?

All purchases are final. Since digital perks are delivered instantly, refunds are only considered in exceptional cases (like a failed delivery we can't resolve). Please contact staff before requesting one.

What payment methods do you accept?

Our store supports all major payment methods including credit/debit cards, PayPal, and more — handled securely through our payment processor.

Are ranks permanent?

Yes! All donor ranks are permanent and tied to your Minecraft account. They work across both PvP and SMP gamemodes.

Do ranks stack?

When you upgrade to a higher rank, you'll automatically receive all the perks of the lower tiers plus the new ones. No need to keep the old ones active.

I bought a rank but it's not showing up. What do I do?

First, try relogging into the server. If it still doesn't appear, head to our Discord and open a ticket — staff will sort it out as soon as possible.
